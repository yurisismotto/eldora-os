# Contributing to Eldora OS

Eldora OS is currently in its Foundation phase (Wave 0).

## External contributions are not open

External contributions (pull requests, patches, code, documentation or
other content) are not accepted during the current phase.

Eldora OS is intended to be an open-source project. Licensing is pending.
Before external contributions open, the project must research and decide:

- the project license (Q-0006);
- the contribution-licensing policy, for example DCO, CLA or another
  mechanism (Q-0007).

See the [Decision Register](docs/adr/DECISION-REGISTER.md) and
[`docs/project/GOVERNANCE.md`](docs/project/GOVERNANCE.md).

## Invited participants

Participants invited by the Project Owner must read:

- `AGENTS.md`;
- `docs/project/CURRENT-STATE.md`;
- `docs/project/GOVERNANCE.md` and `docs/project/DECISION-LIFECYCLE.md`;
- relevant research documents, ADRs and SPECs.

Architectural changes require explicit discussion and appropriate ADRs.

Contributions assisted by AI remain the responsibility of the contributor.
The contributor must review, understand, test, and be able to explain the
submitted work, and must follow the authorship and attribution policy in
`docs/project/GOVERNANCE.md`.

The detailed contribution workflow will be defined before external
contributions open.
