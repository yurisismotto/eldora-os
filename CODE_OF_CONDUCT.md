# Code of Conduct

Status: INTERIM PLACEHOLDER

Eldora OS intends to maintain a respectful, constructive, inclusive, and
professional community.

A definitive Code of Conduct has not been selected. Its selection is tracked
as Q-0011 in the [Decision Register](docs/adr/DECISION-REGISTER.md) and
belongs to Wave 0.6 of the
[Foundation Roadmap](docs/project/FOUNDATION-ROADMAP.md).

Until then, participants are expected to communicate respectfully, focus on
technical and project issues, avoid harassment and personal attacks, and
assume responsibility for their contributions.

This placeholder must not be represented as the final governance policy.
